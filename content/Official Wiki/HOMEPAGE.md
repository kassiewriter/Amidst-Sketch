#### Navigation:
Click the folders in the Explorer (which is located on the left side of this page) to open them, then click a note in that folder to read. Some folders contain subfolders (folders inside folders) for better organization - though I try to keep their usage to a minimum.

#### Features:
- Want to switch to light mode? Click the moon or sun icon on the left tab above the Explorer to swap between viewing modes.

-  Focus mode: Is the Explorer tab distracting you from reading? Click the book icon (located directly to the right of the moon/sun icon) If you're on PC, move your cursor to the center or away from the sides of this page after clicking the book icon once. Hover your cursor over where the Explorer tab would be to unhide it temporarily, until you move your cursor away or click the book icon again.

- Search: To the left of the focus and viewing mode icons, you'll notice a search bar. You can use this to search for Folder titles, Note titles, OR any content within the notes! For example, you can search for a character's name, and all the pages containing their name will show up as a search result. Useful if you're looking for a specific line or phrase.
