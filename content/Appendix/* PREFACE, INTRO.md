
#### A Note from the Author:

*Welcome! This is a writing project I started solo nearing the end of 2025, and I thank all my friends for supporting me and giving advice/critique as time passed and I got to share it to more of those close to me.
It's fascinating to think that someone is able to read this from anywhere in the world, as this story has been taking shape in my head for a few years before I began writing it. I wanted to put it on paper (or notes, in this case) to have the ability to read it from start to finish and share that story to others. It was also overwhelming remembering every single detail without having it written down somewhere, so that was motivating as well.

This story means something to me emotionally, but it'll be a different world everyone finds themselves immersed in. I hope you find joy in reading this absurd adventure of characters I adore... okay maybe not ***all*** *the villains, but they're cool in their own right... And you're free to judge for yourself!*
